Which fan is yours?

Homey sends to many possible fans at once and then asks whether your light changed. That is why about nine questions is enough instead of 189.

Switch the light off with your remote first, and stand where you can see it.

 

About half the answers will be "no". That is normal.

 

 

Is the light on now? Is the light off now? Is the fan running now? Is the fan stopped now? Starting… Working… Trying the other half… Found it — adding the fan… No candidate matched. This fan is already added to Homey.
