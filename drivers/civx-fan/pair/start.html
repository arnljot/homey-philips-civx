Find your fan

Every Civx fan learns its own remote, so Homey has to discover which one yours answers to. It takes about two minutes.

  1. You hold 1 and 2 on the remote, near Homey, so it can listen.
  2. Homey then briefly runs the fan at its lowest speed, and asks whether it reacted.

Stand where you can see the fan, and make sure it is switched off to begin with.

Your existing remote keeps working — nothing is re-paired or overwritten.
